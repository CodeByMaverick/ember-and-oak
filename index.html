Our Story

Rooted in craft.
Grown with love.

Ember & Oak was born from a single conviction — that a great meal is more than food. It is the warmth of the room, the care in the kitchen, and the people sitting across the table.

We source every ingredient from farms within 100 kilometres of our kitchen. Our menu changes with the seasons, never with trends. Come hungry. Leave slowly.
